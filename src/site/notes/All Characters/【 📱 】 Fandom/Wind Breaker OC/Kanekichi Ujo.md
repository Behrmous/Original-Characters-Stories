**✎〕BACKSTORY**   
▸ Japan, Makochi   
    ⤷ Ujo is a 19 year old worker at a small embroidering shop that was created by his mother and grandma when they were still alive. He embroidered some gang symbols, like the Shishitoren tiger, on their jackets. He is the owner of Four Point Star Stitch after becoming orphaned at 17 years old. They are best friends with Momose Takumi, using some of his drawings as inspiration for stitching ideas with his permission. Nirei Akihiko often comes by their shop to info dump about the latest news involving Bofurin. Ujo lets him because he thinks of Nirei as a little brother, also because he enjoys his company.
