✎〕BACKSTORY 
▸ Deika City, Japan
    ⤷ When Juju was born and her parents found out that she was Intersex, they almost immediately sent her to the streets. Not wanting a stain on their perfect “family” image. However, his twin, Juno, would cry any time they tried to “ get rid of “ Juju. The Harunehisa family thought of Juju as a curse. That was, until his quirk developed. It was a rare healing quirk they dubbed, Area of Repair. As they waited years for Juno to develop her quirk, it became apparent it never would show up. Making the pride and joy of the Harunehisa family quirkless. Juju’s parents would try to abandon Juno in public places to try and get rid of her. Juju learned to never leave her alone. Keeping her safe from their  so-called parents. One day, the parents ended up stacking up a debt, causing the two kids to be kidnaped as ransome. The kidnapers ended up realizing that Juju had a good quirk and Juno had none. Making the choice to kill off Juno. The kidnappers achieved this, despite Juju trying so hard to use her quirk to keep her sister alive. When he was saved, he was a hysterical mess. Training to limit their quirk into barely existing. Until, the kidnappers came back and killed the parents. Leaving Juju to run away. Finding Tomura in a park and quickly becoming friends. When the kidnappers caught up, Tomura killed them. Juju, once seeing the people were dust, then healed Tomura's neck he was scratching at. Tomura brought him to the league and soon, Juju was living with him as a roommate. Not joining the league, but being a paid healer for them when they needed it.
