✎〕BACKSTORY 
▸ Japan, Mt. Fujikasane 
    ⤷ Aoimi is a 22 year old kakushi who survived the final selection with Tomioka Giyuu. He was one of the people saved by Sabito and once he was, he decided demon slaying wasn’t for him. Making him change to becoming a kakushi instead. He learned his own breathing technique, Ink Breathing, deriving from water and insect breathing in secret, due to wanting to keep any demon incidents at a minimum when the other kakushi get to the crime sight, as well as in case of a straggler demon getting loose.
