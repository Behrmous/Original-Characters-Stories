### **➂﹞ CHARACTER SPECIFICS**
  
✎〕CHARACTER EXTRAS
「✦」Breathing Style 
    ⤷ Healing Breath (治癒の呼吸, Chiyu no Kokyu)
    
「✦」Breathing Forms
    ⤷  Seven + a Forbidden one
	「 ¹ 」▸**First Form: Gentle Mending Stream** (穏やかな修復の流れ - Odayakana shūfuku no nagare)
	   ⤷  - Basic healing acceleration for minor wounds. Rhythmic breathing that increases blood flow to injured areas. Nazoji throws multiple bo shuriken in a gentle arc. When they pierce demon flesh, they inject microscopic amounts of Hichi, enough to confuse and causing their regeneration to slow. Against humans, the needles seek out pressure points that promote natural healing or slow down the foe.
	「 ² 」▸**Second Form: Memories Embrace** (思い出に包まれて - Omoide ni tsutsumarete)
	   ⤷  Uses blood-infused weapon strikes to confuse demons. Weapon techniques that prioritize contact over fatal cuts. They throw bo shuriken with pinpoint accuracy at specific nerve centers on demons, not to kill, but to temporarily restore their human memories and emotions. This often causes demons to hesitate or even weep, creating openings for a mercy killing.
	「 ³ 」▸**Third Form: Gentle Rain** (優しい雨 - Yasashī ame)
	   ⤷  A soft, continuous breathing pattern slowly mending minor wounds and fatigue. Nazoji deliberately their weapon across their arm, allowing his Hichi to coat multiple bo shuriken. They target specific nerve clusters to temporarily paralyze demons without permanent harm.
	「 ⁴ 」▸**Fourth Form: Blood Moon Dance** ( 血の月舞 - Chi no Tsuki Mai )
	   ⤷  A graceful spinning technique where Nazoji launches multiple bo shuriken in a circular pattern while their blood creates protective barriers. This form excels at defending allies while simultaneously attacking enemies.
	「 ⁵ 」▸**Fifth Form: Phoenix Rising** (鳳凰が舞い上がる - Hō-ō ga maiagaru)
	   ⤷  A close-combat technique where Nazoji uses their enhanced healing to absorb damage while delivering devastating pressure point attacks at point-blank range.
	「 ⁶ 」▸**Sixth Form: Final Rest** (最後の休息 - Saigo no kyūsoku)
	   ⤷  Slow and Calm breathe, like you are falling asleep, this technique ensures a demon's death is instantaneous and filled with their most cherished human memories. Nazoji covers their Bo Shuriken with blood to give them final memories of being a human and aims for the demons neck.
	「 ⁷ 」▸**Seventh Form: Rebirth Through Sacrifice** (犠牲を通しての再生 - Gisei o tōshite no saisei)
	   ⤷  Ultimate technique—major self-injury to produce large quantities of healing blood. Dangerous breathing pattern that accelerates healing to dangerous levels.
	「 ⁸ 」▸**Forbidden Form: Sunrise Purification** (日の出の浄化 - Hinode no jōka)
	   ⤷  - Nazoji's most dangerous technique, where they can offer their blood to a newly-turned demon, creating a temporary barrier against the demonic transformation—though this comes with terrible risks.
