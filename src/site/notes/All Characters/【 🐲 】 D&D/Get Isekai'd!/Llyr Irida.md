✎〕BACKSTORY
▸ Costa Rica to The USA
    ⤷ In the year of 2003, a young baby boy was born, named Llyr Irida. Llyr was born into a modest family, not quite 100% stable financially but not poor either. More of a so called “ Lower Class “ family. He was born into a small family with a young, sick mother: Ceri Lloyd. He looked just like her, developing her Vitiligo from his mother. She suffered with Interstitial Lung Disease (ILD) from a young age. Her disease got worse after her pregnancy with Llyr.

Even with her weak lungs, she took her family to trips to the sea often. Showing them her love for the ocean and it’s life. Leonor, Llyr’s older sister, wasn’t much of a water person. So she and Wayner, her and Llyr’s father, would stay home and read instead of going outside. Leaving Ceri and Iwan ( his uncle ) to go to the beach together. Iwan would tell Llyr about his stories of diving, bringing up sunken machinery for Llyr to tinker with when he visits from Wales during the winter.

Llyr grew into a fascination with coral reefs and messing with metal around the age of three. Wayner teaching him how to put things together and take them apart to see how they worked. Even if Llyr was closer to his mother, he still respected his father for teaching him about some of his engendering work, when he is back from working at an Oil Rig.

When Llyr was four, his mother announced her last pregnancy with his soon to be little sister, Ligia. In the beginning of the pregnancy, she was fine but, as time passed and Ligia grew inside of her, her lungs got weaker and weaker. Soon, she was in labor on three days after her birthday, February 28th. Labor was straining her and her ability to breathe. After 13 hours of pushing, Ligia was born. Ceri was on risk watch for 48 hours before Llyr, Leonor, Iwan, and Wayner could see her. They day they finally could, she was pale and fatigued all the time. She lasted a week after labor before dying from the strain on her body.

Iwan stayed with the Irida’s to help them plan his sisters funeral. Wayner went into a deep depression. Silently resenting his youngest child, leaving Leonor and Llyr to take up the mantle of parents for a few months until Iwan confronted his brother in law about it. Wayner got out of his funk and began being a father again. Prompting Iwan to think everything would be fine and return to Wales.

Everything was fine for a few years. Llyr meeting his best friend, Fidel Pilar one winter with Iwan visiting to take him diving. They hit it off instantly and became inseparable. They were tied to the him from then on. Diving together with Fidel’s Step Mother every weekend. They would establish a routine- Fidel would get off of school and meet Llyr at the local diving spot with his Step Mom, They would dive, Eat some shaved ice, and then hang out until sundown. Sometimes, Ligia would join them when it was Llyr’s day to watch her.

Everything began getting worse when He was 15. Wayner started to drink, heavily. He slaked on his parenting duties, getting fired from his Oil Rig Engineering job as the drinking got more and more frequent until he was almost never sober. He became angry and started pushing Llyr around. Resenting his only son for looking like his deceased wife. Leonor and Llyr were prompted to get jobs to hold the house down. Taking turns watching Ligia just in case Wayner did something.

During a call with his uncle Iwan, Llyr told him what was going on at home. Iwan instantly got on a plane and showed up one day to see Wayner finally getting angry enough to try and grab Leonor. Llyr got in the way and took it and Iwan called the authorities. Wayner was taken into custody and his rights to his children were taken from him and relinquished to Iwan.

It takes Wayner three years to get semi-sober enough to have some monitored visits with his kids. Ligia and Leonor refused to see him, Llyr only goes to see him out of obligation and guilt. Wayner never gets the rights to his kids back. Leonor moves out of the house and goes to Wales with Ligia and Iwan when Llyr is accepted into Collage. He moves to the USA, keeping in contact with Fidel, his uncle, and sisters. He finally cuts Wayner out of his life when he moves to the US.

During his time in collage he is friendly with people but never calls anyone friends. He is mostly focused on his studies rather then going to parties and making friends. He knows people by the faces, not thier names. During his summer breaks he and his family go back to Costa Rica and dive together. ( Except Leonor. ) He reunites with Fidel and tells him everything he has learned during school. ( Fidel develops a small crush on Llyr, but never says anything. )

When summer is over for the last time before he gets his AAS, He and Fidel makes plans to live together in Costa Rica when he gets back. He says goodbye, not knowing this is the last time he will see his best friend. The last time before getting Isekaied.
