✎〕BACKSTORY 
▸ St. Clara, Illinois USA
    ⤷ Ecru was born into a family with little wealth. Shoplifting and Drug dealing were common in their household. In fact, their mother began teaching them how to grow and produce decent quality weed and keep it a secret from the pigs. (police, as the family had nicknamed them). Ecru's father was rarely present. He only dared to show his face if he brought home one of the three things; Food, Water, or at LEAST $300 in cash. He was also a drug dealer, however he sold more illegal substances to try and get a bigger pretty penny. Despite breaking the law ever since they were able to walk, their family was kind. Mother was gentle but realistic. Never sugar coating the family's financial issues. Though their father was rarely at their apartment, he made it a routine to drop off stolen trinkets for them. It was just the three of them from the very beginning. One, more expensive trinket was a small instrument called a Kalimba (Or Thumb Piano). It looked hand made, with a small lanyard on the back allowing them to hang it on their neck like a necklace. All three members of the Ivory household had exceptional talent in creative trades. The Mother, Wren, could've been a famous embroiderer with how perfect and neat her embroidery always turns out. The Father, Otto, would've made a phenomenal wood carver due to his carefully carved small designs. Ecru, just like their parents, was good at something. When they were little, they would always be sitting in front of the radio humming harmonies to the songs playing. Adding their own little twists on the notes as they would work on the weed production. Once their father left the Kalimba on their dresser with a little note explaining what it is, Ecru was never seen without it around their neck. It soon became apparent how good Ecru was at the instrument. They would go and make a habit of sitting in a small park and play their Kalimba for the people passing by. Sometimes even getting a small surprise tip from the local children. Adding whatever they did make into their own secret savings. It wasn’t that much of a secret because both their father and mother would sometimes add a dollar to it thinking they wouldn’t notice. Soon the “secret” savings was enough for Ecru to go out and purchase their own cheap Violin with some sheets of music. Ecru became so good at both the Violin and the Kalimba that they were receiving more and more frequent tips for the music they played. Evening writing some of their own! This continued all into the end of middle school, their music bringing in enough income that their mother soon forbade them from selling drugs like the rest of the family. Wren didn’t want her child ending up like them, she wanted Ecru to be successful with their talent. Not wanting the family's weed business to risk Ecru’s future in case of getting busted.  However, nearing the end of Ecru’s high school career, Both Wren and Otto got busted with being in possession of Weed and Heroin. Soon being sent to prison as Ecru watches from the neighbors window. The neighbors were loose friends with the Ivory’s and became temporary housing for them as they were already legally an adult. Allowing Ecru to use their spare bedroom until they finish their last year of high school.
