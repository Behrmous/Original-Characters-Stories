    ⤷ Ode was made by a sketchy toy maker who secretly puts shards of an artifact in the creations that she makes. Allowing for the creation she made to have the ability to use magic and use it. The sketchy toy maker was trying to create toys who secretly act as a body guard for the person or people they are gifted to. Ari's parents bought Ode from the sketchy toy maker thinking they were just a toy for their kid. Ari found out that Ode was a Threadborn almost as soon as Ode was gifted to them. Ode swore to protect Ari from harm once finding out that they are a Warlock. Ode's role was to protect, entertain, and watch after Ari when the parents were gone ( or something similar ) and throughout the time they were together, Ode would train to Lv 5 to protect Ari better than if they were a lower level. Ode would study magic in its free time to become strong enough to keep protecting Ari. When Ari decides to run away, Ode follows without question to keep protecting them.
