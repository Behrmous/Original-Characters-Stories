✎〕BACKSTORY

    ⤷ Behrri is a Magic Mud Cryptid that is forever stuck in a time loop. Living the past year over and over again trying to escape. They attempt to get out of the time loop in various ways in the beginning ( Suicide, Meeting different people, doing different things ). They will try to escape the time loop in whatever ways they can. Unless it goes against their own moral code, which they have somehow kept held of throughout the loops. Sometimes meeting other people or creatures from different universes in time breaks. Allowing freedom in another universe until a year has passed.
