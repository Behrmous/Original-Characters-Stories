✎〕BACKSTORY 
▸ Unova Region, Nacrene City 
    ⤷ Bodhi was just a little kid when their guardian brought home a Pokémon egg that they found on the side of the road. Bodhi was very curious about the egg and ended up spending a lot of time with it. Reading to it, cleaning it, and even singing to it in a quiet voice. The egg was very loved. Bodhi had to fight to keep that egg in the house and tried to convince their guardian that they could take care of a Pokémon. Eventually the guardian caved and bought a cherish ball for Bodhi to catch the Pokémon in when it hatched. Over the days it seemed as if the Pokémon in the egg would hatch later than other eggs of its season, making Bodhi worried. They took the egg to the nearest Pokémon center during a slight storm, not letting their guardian know where they were going. Getting lost in the storm and soon surrounded by wild Pokémon, the egg then began to hatch. A blinding light shining from the egg. An Togepi soon emerged and the wild Pokémon ran away because of the light. The Togepi was a runt, small and weak. Soon Bodhi made it to the Pokémon center and they took care of the Togepi. The Togepi allowed them to catch it once it was in a more stable condition.
